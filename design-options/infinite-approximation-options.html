House Slytherin, analysed for this site

What the books establish about the house, and what each point becomes on the page. The site will not use the house's name, crest or any film artwork: Harry Potter is a trademark, and every drawing on this page is my own.

TraitFrom the booksOn the site
ColoursGreen and silver.Deep green surfaces, silver lines and text accents; a brighter green only where the eye should go.
AnimalThe serpent, after the founder Salazar Slytherin, a Parselmouth.Trajectories already wind like snakes. The logo, a loading animation and the right-angle marks can carry a serpent motif.
Common roomA long, low stone room in the dungeons under the lake, lit by greenish lamps hanging on chains, with a greenish light from the water (Chamber of Secrets, ch. 12).Dark mode becomes "under the lake": stone-dark background, green light from above, a soft ripple.
ValuesThe Sorting Hat names ambition and cunning; resourcefulness is also given.Sharp, precise, no clutter: thin silver rules, exact numbers, and a tool that fixes your parameters for you (resourceful).
CharacterOld, proud, formal.A classical inscription face (Cinzel or similar) for headings; STIX Two stays for the maths, which it sets best.
RiskGreen on black can turn murky and hard to read.Every green and silver below shows its contrast; text keeps 4.5:1 or more (WCAG AA).

Source: J. K. Rowling's novels (Philosopher's Stone, ch. 7, the Sorting Hat's song; Chamber of Secrets, ch. 12, the common room). I paraphrased the descriptions; nothing is quoted.
